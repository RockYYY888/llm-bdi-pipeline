\begin{abstract}
Generalized planning learns reusable behavior for problem families, whereas
Belief--Desire--Intention (BDI) agents execute plan libraries. Connecting
generalized planning to BDI execution exposes a representation gap:
singleton-goal evidence may omit modules for subsidiary achievements and does
not establish sound temporal composition. We introduce GP2PL (Generalized
Planning to Plan Libraries). Its typed input contract maps controlled
natural-language requests to bound finite-trace temporal-logic query
specifications consistent with the planning domain. Its compiler maps
generalized-planning evidence and action models into certified BDI plan
libraries. GP2PL validates and lifts evidence, generates schema-derived modules
for producible goals, selects a compact closed atomic core, and appends
DFA-guided query controllers without relearning that core. Within the supported
candidate language and query fragment, GP2PL accepts only branches and
controllers satisfying its soundness conditions. Atomic coverage averages
98.7\% across five seeds; all 475 translations are DFA-language equivalent to
their gold formulas, and all 6,140 admitted temporal executions pass
validation.
\end{abstract}
